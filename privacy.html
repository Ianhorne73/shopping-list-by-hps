About this policy

This policy covers Shopping List by HPS, its help website and information provided to us for support. The add-on is independent of ServiceM8. ServiceM8 and GitHub also have their own privacy policies for the services they provide.

Information the add-on uses

When an authorised user opens a list, the add-on requests the selected ServiceM8 job's identifier, number and address, its material-line identifiers, descriptions, quantities and catalogue references, and linked catalogue item codes. API responses may contain additional fields; the add-on only uses fields needed for these functions. Job addresses and free-text descriptions may contain personal information entered by the account holder.

ServiceM8 provides temporary OAuth credentials and invocation context to authorise requests. The add-on does not ask for your ServiceM8 password or deliberately store access tokens in shopping-list data.

Saved shopping state contains line identifiers, material descriptions, quantities, catalogue references and collected/excluded status. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

Purposes and responsibilities

Information is used to display job materials, retain shopping status, retrieve saved updates for authorised users of the same ServiceM8 account, copy or print lists, diagnose errors and respond to support and privacy requests. The current add-on does not use job data for advertising, marketing profiles or automated decisions about people.

The ServiceM8 account holder controls the source job records and staff access and is responsible for an appropriate basis to use those records. We process job information on the account holder's behalf to provide the requested shopping-list service. For direct support correspondence, we use the information to answer the request, administer the service and meet applicable legal obligations. Where relevant, these purposes rely on providing the requested service or our legitimate interests in supporting it, subject to individuals' rights.

Storage, access and disclosures

Processing runs in ServiceM8-hosted functions. Shopping state is stored in ServiceM8's private add-on storage; earlier account-level state may also be read during migration to job-level storage. The current implementation has no separate external customer database. The add-on does not change quotes, invoices, source job material quantities or inventory levels.

Saved status can be retrieved by authorised users of the same ServiceM8 account. ServiceM8 and its infrastructure providers process information for hosting, authentication, API access and storage. Error messages and event names are logged to ServiceM8-managed diagnostics; the add-on does not deliberately log full job records or tokens. Support correspondence is processed through our email service and may be accessed by people handling the request. If we need to involve a provider to resolve an issue, we will limit the information shared to what is necessary.

The public website and artwork use GitHub Pages and GitHub. Requests to these services may disclose technical connection information, such as IP address and browser information, to GitHub and its providers. The add-on does not deliberately send job records or shopping state to GitHub. The public repository contains documentation and artwork, not customer records. There is no advertising or analytics integration in the current add-on.

We do not sell job or shopping-list information. Information may be disclosed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or lawful request.

International processing

We operate from Australia. ServiceM8, GitHub and their providers may process information internationally, including outside your country. GitHub describes international processing, including in the United States, in its privacy statement. ServiceM8's hosting arrangements depend on the account and service. Their policies describe their transfer arrangements; this policy does not promise that all information stays in one country. Contact us for information about a particular account or support request.

Retention and deletion

Shopping state does not currently have an automatic expiry. Unticking an item removes its stored collection status; restoring an excluded item removes its exclusion. Entries for deleted material lines are pruned when a later shopping-status update is saved for the job. Source records in ServiceM8 are managed separately by the account holder.

Disconnecting the add-on should not be assumed to delete all existing shopping data. Email our privacy contact to request access, correction or deletion. We will verify your authority and coordinate with the account administrator or ServiceM8 where required. Provider-managed logs and backups are subject to provider retention arrangements, and technical or legal limits may affect immediate deletion.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, follow-up issues, disputes and applicable record-keeping obligations. Relevant criteria include whether the matter remains open, whether the information is needed for an ongoing issue or claim, and any applicable legal retention requirement. Contact us about deletion of a particular support conversation. The add-on does not set a separate expiry for ServiceM8-managed diagnostic logs.

Your choices, rights and complaints

You can correct source information in ServiceM8, change collection or exclusion status, stop using the add-on or disconnect it. Printed and copied lists are outside the add-on's control; protect them when sharing or disposing of them.

Depending on applicable law and our role, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Email ian.horne@hireahubby.com.au to make a request or complaint. We may need to confirm identity and authority and may refer requests about source job records to the responsible account holder. We will respond within applicable legal time limits and explain any limits on fulfilling a request.

You may also complain to your local privacy regulator, including the OAIC in Australia, the relevant privacy commissioner in Canada or New Zealand, the ICO in the United Kingdom, or your local European supervisory authority where applicable. This policy does not limit statutory rights.

Security and payment information

The add-on uses HTTPS and temporary OAuth credentials for ServiceM8 requests. Protect your ServiceM8 account, device, printouts and copied lists. Do not send passwords or access tokens in support messages or post customer information in public GitHub issues.

Shopping List by HPS is free and does not collect payment-card details or charge a subscription fee. Separate ServiceM8 account charges remain subject to ServiceM8's terms.

Changes and provider policies

Updates will be published here with a revision date. Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.

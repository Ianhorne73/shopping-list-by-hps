Shopping List by HPS

Your job materials, ready for your next supplies run.

Free to use. Coming soon to the ServiceM8 Add-on Store.

Everything you need for your materials run

Turn the materials already added to a ServiceM8 job into a handy shopping list. See what you need, tick items as you collect them, and keep track of what’s left.

  • Product details at a glance: descriptions, quantities and item codes where available.
  • Clear progress: collected items stay visible with a strikethrough.
  • A list for each job: keep materials organised wherever you’re working.
  • Easy to share: refresh for your team’s saved updates, copy remaining items or print your list.

Open your shopping list

Once the add-on is activated in your ServiceM8 account:

  1. Open a job.
  2. Choose More → Shopping List by HPS.
  3. Review your materials and tick items as you collect them.

On the Online Dashboard, the list opens over your job card. On iPhone or iPad, the job action opens a web page. Use Edit Actions to add it to your favourites. You’ll need an internet connection.

Tick, exclude and restore

Tick an item when you collect it. It stays visible with a strikethrough. Untick it if you still need to buy it.

Exclude items you already have or don’t need to purchase. Find them in the Excluded view and choose Restore to return them to your list. Your original job materials stay in place.

Use Refresh to see saved updates from another device. Coordinate with your team when updating the same job, as simultaneous changes can overwrite one another.

Copy or print your list

Copy list copies the remaining materials, including their codes and quantities. Print includes the job number and address so you know which job the list belongs to. Available printing and clipboard options depend on your device and browser.

Useful things to know

Quantities come directly from your job’s materials. Check what you already have on hand before shopping. Descriptions containing “labour” or “labor” are hidden automatically; you can exclude other services manually.

When an item’s description, quantity or catalogue reference changes, its shopping status resets so you can review it again. Items with zero, negative or invalid quantities are left off the list.

My list is empty

Check that the job has materials with quantities greater than zero. Items marked as labour or excluded won’t appear in the main list. After editing job materials, select Refresh.

I can’t open the list

Reopen it from the job’s More menu. If it’s missing, ask your account administrator to check that the add-on is activated and its permissions are approved.

A change hasn’t saved

Select Refresh and check the item again. If an error appears, retry when your connection is available. Contact support if the problem continues.

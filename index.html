Shopping List by HPS

Materials already on your job, ready for your next supplies run.

Pre-release documentation. Public store submission is being prepared. A reported issue with retaining checked and excluded items on some jobs is under investigation. If a save error appears, refresh and check the result before relying on it.

Get started

The add-on uses your ServiceM8 account and does not require a separate login. Once activated by your administrator, open a job and choose More → Shopping List by HPS.

On the Online Dashboard, the list opens over the job card. In the iPhone/iPad app, the job action opens a web page. If the action is missing, check activation and use Edit Actions to add it to your job-action favourites. Internet access is required.

Read your list

Each shopping list relates to one job and displays material descriptions, quantities and catalogue item codes when available. Add or edit materials on the job in ServiceM8, then refresh the list.

Quantities come directly from job material lines. Stock on hand is not deducted. Lines containing the whole word “labour” or “labor” are hidden automatically. Other service lines can be excluded manually. Zero, negative or invalid quantities are omitted.

Collect, exclude and restore

  • Tick an item when collected. It remains visible with a strikethrough. Untick it to mark it as still needed.
  • Use Exclude for services or items already on hand. This does not delete the source material from the job.
  • Choose Excluded to find excluded items and restore them.
  • Use Refresh to retrieve saved changes from other devices.

If an item's description, quantity or catalogue reference changes, its saved shopping status resets for review. Coordinate simultaneous edits to the same job: the latest save can replace concurrent changes.

Copy and print

Copy list copies outstanding materials with their codes and quantities. Print includes the job number and address. Clipboard and printing options depend on your browser and device.

Troubleshooting

If access is denied, reopen the list from the job and check the add-on's permissions. If ServiceM8 is busy, retry later. If the list is empty, check that the job contains positive-quantity materials that are not labour or excluded.

If a save or reload error appears, do not assume the change was retained. Refresh and verify the result. During the pre-release period, contact the administrator who supplied the add-on. Public support contact details and the developer privacy policy will be added before release.

Data and permissions

The add-on reads job information, material lines and catalogue item codes, and writes private shopping status in ServiceM8 add-on storage. It does not change quotes, invoices, job material quantities or inventory levels.

Shopping List by HPS is an independent add-on. It is not developed or supported by ServiceM8. This help page is pre-release documentation, not a privacy policy.
